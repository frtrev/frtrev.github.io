Privacy Policy

Arthropod Invasion is published by Francisco Trevino under NeoTheOne. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Local information

The game stores settings and gameplay information, such as the high score, locally on the player’s device. This information is used only to provide game functionality and is not transmitted to the developer or third parties.

Services and permissions

The game does not contain advertising, analytics, user accounts, location tracking, or in-app purchases. It does not request access to contacts, photos, the camera, microphone, or location.

Children’s privacy

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other players.

Changes and contact

If a future version changes these practices, this policy will be updated before that version is released. Questions may be sent to ftrevino@yahoo.com.
