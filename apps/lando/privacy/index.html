LandO Privacy Policy

LandO is a property mapping and planning app published by Francisco Trevino under NeoTheOne. Privacy and support contact: ftrevino@yahoo.com.

Your property data

Property names, descriptions, boundary coordinates, areas, distance measurements, preferences and conversations are stored in the app's private storage on your device. LandO uses Firebase Authentication for registration and sign-in. Google processes account credentials and related authentication information. LandO stores your Firebase user ID, selected demo plan, AI agreement version and acceptance/withdrawal timestamps, and usage counters on its backend. Property data and chat archives remain on this device; account sign-in does not synchronize them between devices. There are no advertising or developer-operated analytics services in the app. Third-party services below may process their own operational data.

Location, maps and address search

With your permission, LandO accesses your location while the app is in use to center the map. Location permission is optional; you can search or pan the map without granting it and revoke it in your device settings. LandO does not request background location, microphone or camera access. Google Maps and Google Places receive map requests, visible map/location context and address searches, and may process IP addresses, device/app information and diagnostics under Google's terms. Maps and address searches require an internet connection.

Optional Pro assistant and internet research

When a Pro user agrees to share assistant data and sends a question, their question, recent conversation and app-calculated property context are sent over HTTPS through LandO's backend to Google Gemini. Property context may contain precise coordinates and names or descriptions you entered. LandO manages the provider credential on its server; users do not supply or receive AI keys. Backend requests verify your Firebase identity and plan and update usage counters. Application code does not log conversation bodies or provider credentials. Hosting services may process operational connection information and logs under their terms. Gemini processes submitted data under the terms applicable to LandO's provider account. Before the first AI request, LandO presents a full AI disclosure and limitations acknowledgement and asks for explicit permission to share the described data with Google Gemini. Acceptance is recorded once per account and agreement version, including across reinstalls and devices. You can decline and continue mapping, or review the agreement and withdraw permission in Settings → AI agreement. Material changes to the recipient or sharing terms require a new agreement.

Internet research is managed by LandO and may send queries containing location or context from your question or conversation to Google's search services. Opening a source link sends you to that site's own service and privacy policy. The current app does not offer on-device AI or provider selection. Data already submitted through previous versions remains subject to those services' terms.

AI-answer reports and support

You can report an answer from inside the app. When you press Send report, the selected reason and any comments you enter are sent through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) to the LandO support inbox at ftrevino@yahoo.com. The answer itself is included only if you select the checkbox. A report does not automatically include your full chat, property database, API keys, location or a contact email. Content you choose to include may itself contain property information. FormSubmit and the email provider process the submission and connection information under their own policies. Do not include passwords, API keys or information you do not want to share. Reports and support correspondence are retained as needed to investigate and resolve issues, and may be retained for security or legal obligations. Contact support to request deletion of identifiable report or support information. LandO does not control third-party retention.

Backup and sharing

Export creates a readable file containing your properties, coordinates, conversations and preferences. You decide whether to save it or share it with another app. API keys and downloaded models are excluded. Anyone with the exported file can read its contents. Copies you save or send are managed by you and the services you choose. Review their access and retention settings.

Your choices and deletion

You can delete individual properties and conversations, sign out, revoke location permission, or use Android Settings to clear LandO storage. Uninstalling the Android app removes its private app data. On iOS, use Delete App rather than Offload App to remove local app files; Keychain entries may survive reinstallation. Device backups may retain prior app data according to your backup settings. Exported files and data already sent to third-party services are not removed by deleting local app data. Contact support to request deletion of your LandO account and associated backend plan and usage records. Signing out does not delete your account or local properties. AI permission is tied to your account and agreement version, rather than to a backup file or installation. Restoring a backup does not grant permission for another account. Withdrawal blocks subsequent AI requests and does not undo data already shared.

Security and important limits

Network services use HTTPS. Device storage protections depend on your operating system and device security; exported backup files are not encrypted by LandO. No method of storage or transmission provides an absolute guarantee. AI answers, prices and map-derived measurements are planning estimates, not legal surveys, verified contractor quotes, appraisals, or guarantees of ownership or permit approval. Confirm important decisions with qualified professionals.

Audience and changes

LandO is intended for general adult property planning and is not directed to children. Do not enter children's personal information. This policy may change when features or data handling change; the effective date will be updated. Questions about privacy can be sent to ftrevino@yahoo.com.
