Planning guidance

Measurements & AI limitations

LandO helps you explore possibilities. Its map geometry, calculations and assistant answers need to be checked against the conditions on your property.

Boundaries and measurements

Points placed on satellite imagery are user-defined planning geometry. Imagery alignment, image age, terrain and point placement affect accuracy. Displayed areas and distances do not replace a professional survey, title records or verified property lines. Do not treat map drawings as proof of ownership.

AI-generated guidance

LandO’s assistant can make mistakes, omit context or produce incorrect calculations and explanations. A local model may be less capable than a cloud model. Check units, assumptions, quantities and cited sources. The assistant provides planning guidance rather than professional legal, engineering, structural, financial or surveying services.

Internet research and installation prices

Online information may be old, incomplete or unrelated to your location. A source link does not guarantee the answer accurately represents the source. Costs vary with materials, labor, access, terrain, removal, permits and project scope. Obtain current local contractor quotes before budgeting or purchasing.

Permits and physical work

Confirm local requirements, setbacks, easements, utilities and site conditions with the appropriate authorities and qualified professionals before digging or building. Never rely on a map or assistant answer to locate underground utilities or determine whether work is safe.
