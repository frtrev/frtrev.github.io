LandO help

Support

For questions, technical issues, feedback or privacy requests, email ftrevino@yahoo.com. LandO is published by Francisco Trevino under NeoTheOne.

What to include

  • LandO version, Android version and phone model.
  • The action you took, what you expected and what happened.
  • Relevant error text or a screenshot with private details removed.
Keep private information private. Do not send API keys, passwords or signing files. Remove personal addresses and coordinates from screenshots unless they are necessary and you choose to share them. Send a property backup only if requested and after reviewing its contents.

Report an AI answer

Use Report answer beneath the assistant’s answer. The current app sends the selected reason and comments through FormSubmit; attaching the answer is optional. If the app cannot confirm submission, it will show an error. You can also email support with a description and any excerpt you choose to include. Do not assume a failed report was delivered. See the privacy policy for report handling.

Back up or move to another phone

Use LandO’s import/export data feature to export your data, then save the file somewhere you control. On the other installation, import that backup. Export before uninstalling or clearing app storage. API keys and downloaded model weights are not included; configure those separately after restoring.

Backup files contain readable property coordinates and conversations. Protect the file and share it only with people or services you trust. Restoring data requires permission again before assistant content is shared with online services.

Maps and location

Location access is optional. Allow it while using the app to start near your location, or search and move around the map manually. Maps and address search require internet access. Check device location settings and connectivity if your position or map is unavailable.

AI setup and connectivity

Choose your assistant in AI settings. Gemini and OpenAI require your own provider API key and may incur charges. The on-device Qwen option requires a compatible 64-bit Android installation and an approximately 508 MiB download. To answer locally offline after the download, turn off Query the internet. Online research needs connectivity even when the assistant itself runs locally.

Manage or delete your data

  • Delete individual properties and their conversations using LandO’s property and conversation controls.
  • Remove stored AI keys and the downloaded local model in AI settings.
  • Clear LandO’s app storage in Android Settings to remove local data, or uninstall the Android app. Export a backup first if you want to keep your work.
  • Delete exported backups separately from Files, email, cloud storage or other apps where you saved them.
  • For identifiable support messages or submitted reports, email ftrevino@yahoo.com with enough information to locate the submission. Do not send identity documents or secrets. Some information may be retained for security or legal obligations.

There is no LandO account to delete. Deleting local data does not delete information already handled by Maps, AI, search or reporting providers. Use those providers’ controls for their independently retained data.

Important limits

LandO does not establish legal boundaries, ownership, permits or a professional appraisal. Read the planning and AI limitations before relying on measurements or advice.
