LandO help

Support

For questions, technical issues, feedback or privacy requests, email ftrevino@yahoo.com. LandO is published by Francisco Trevino under NeoTheOne.

Get started and sign in

LandO supports Android, iPhone and iPad. The current app requires an email/password account. Register or sign in, then choose Free or Pro demo. Use Forgot password? on the sign-in screen to request a reset email. If you previously signed in on an older iOS build, the updated app may ask you to sign in once again.

Free and Pro demo

Free supports one saved property and does not include AI. Pro demo supports unlimited properties and the managed assistant. Both are selectable demo plans in this build: there are no purchases, billing or verified paid subscriptions. Change your selection in Settings → Account & plan. Switching to Free preserves existing properties but restricts additional property creation and assistant access. A Free account cannot import a backup containing more than one property.

Map controls and adding points

Search for an address or move the map manually. Location permission is optional; the location button centers on your position when permission is granted. The Saved map view button restores the position, zoom, rotation and tilt recorded when you explicitly saved the property.

To insert a boundary point, unlock boundary editing and choose Add point. After the hint, tap between the two points where you want the new point. LandO inserts it at the tapped location between the nearest pair and ends the flow. Tap Add point again before placing it to cancel. When editing an area or distance layer, Add point inserts into that layer. Use the Details/More/Hide control to show or hide property information; Layers manages saved areas and distance measurements.

Use the ruler for a temporary measurement

  1. Tap the ruler below the map-view and label buttons, then acknowledge the hint.
  2. Tap locations to draw a path. The length label updates as you add or move points.
  3. With at least two points placed, tap the last point to finish. LandO saves the temporary measurement locally for that property and hides its point handles.
  4. Tap the line or length label to show its points and edit again. Drag individual points to adjust them; tap the last point to finish and save the changes.
  5. Tap the ruler again to clear the temporary measurement. There is only one temporary measurement per property. The ruler changes color while measuring or editing is active.

A temporary measurement does not require a name or an entry in the saved Layers list. To see a hidden length label, turn map labels back on, or tap the line itself to reopen editing.

Show hints again

Each hint has its own Do not show this again option. In Settings → Map & preferences, check Show hints to re-enable dismissed hints. You can then dismiss each hint individually again. Unchecking Show hints disables the map hints.

Use the managed assistant

Select Pro demo and open Ask LandO from a property. The current assistant runs through LandO’s authenticated backend and Google Gemini; it requires internet access. You do not enter a provider API key or download an on-device model. Free accounts do not have assistant access.

Before your first request, read the AI disclosure, check the acknowledgement and explicitly agree. The acceptance is stored for your account and agreement version, including across reinstalls and devices. You can decline and continue mapping, or review and withdraw permission in Settings → AI agreement. Restoring a backup does not grant another account permission. Withdrawal blocks later AI requests and cannot recall data already sent.

The assistant may research information online. Check source links, units, assumptions and calculations before relying on an answer. If a question takes time, Keep waiting continues the current calculation; Stop and rephrase stops it so you can revise your question. A new retry starts a new attempt.

Report an AI answer

Use Report answer beneath an answer. The app sends the selected reason and comments through FormSubmit; attaching the selected answer is optional. If submission cannot be confirmed, it shows an error. Do not assume a failed report was delivered. You can also email support with the details and excerpt you choose to include. See the privacy policy for handling of reports.

Back up or move to another phone

Use Settings → Backup & restore to export your local property data, conversations and preferences. Save the file somewhere you control. On the other installation, sign in to the intended account and import the backup. Sign-in alone does not synchronize properties or conversations between devices. Each account has separate local storage.

Export before uninstalling, clearing app storage or changing installations. Backups are readable and contain coordinates and conversations. Protect them and review what you share. Restore replaces the destination’s local data after confirmation; export that data first if you want to keep it. Account authentication and backend AI consent are managed separately from the backup. Legacy API keys and downloaded model weights are excluded.

Maps and location troubleshooting

Maps and address searches need connectivity. Check your connection and device location settings if map tiles, search or your position are unavailable. You can decline location permission and pan or search manually. Saved geometry and app calculations remain local, but sign-in and server-backed features require connectivity.

Request account deletion

To request deletion of your LandO account and its associated backend plan, AI agreement and usage records, email ftrevino@yahoo.com from the account’s email address. State that you want your LandO account deleted. Support may need to verify that you control the account; never send your password, authentication codes or identity documents in the initial request.

Signing out, uninstalling the app or deleting a property does not delete your Firebase account or backend records. Account deletion does not remove local files on every device, exported backups, or data independently retained by third-party providers. Manage those copies separately using the steps below. Identifiable support/report records may need a separate deletion request and may be retained for security or legal obligations. The current app uses this support-request process for account deletion.

Manage or delete local data

  • Delete individual properties and conversations using the app’s controls. The ruler clears its temporary measurement when tapped again.
  • Sign out through Account & plan when you want to end the session. Signing out preserves local property data.
  • On Android, clear LandO’s app storage in device Settings or uninstall the app to remove its private local files.
  • On iOS, use Delete App to remove local app files. Offload App preserves data, and Keychain entries and device backups may survive deletion. A fresh installation signs out a retained Firebase session before allowing access.
  • Delete exported backups separately from Files, email, cloud storage and any apps or devices where you kept them.
  • For support messages or reports you submitted, email ftrevino@yahoo.com with enough information to locate them. Do not send secrets or unnecessary personal data.

Data already shared with Google Maps, Gemini, reporting services or linked websites is subject to those services’ retention policies. Removing local data does not recall those submissions.

What to include when contacting support

  • The LandO version/build, Android or iOS version, and device model.
  • The action you took, what you expected and what happened.
  • Relevant error text or a screenshot with private details removed.
Keep private information private. Do not send API keys, passwords, authentication codes or signing files. Remove private addresses and coordinates from screenshots unless needed and you choose to share them. Send a property backup only when requested and after reviewing its contents.

Important limits

LandO does not establish legal boundaries, ownership, permits or a professional appraisal. Read the planning and AI limitations before relying on measurements or advice.
