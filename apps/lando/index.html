Map and explore

Find addresses, explore satellite maps, save properties and edit their boundaries. Lock coordinates to protect your layout from accidental changes. Location permission is optional.

Measure and arrange

Save multiple areas and distance paths per property. Show or hide layers, tap labels to change units, and use imperial or metric measurements. Move, resize and rotate custom areas as you plan.

Ask LandO

Discuss your property and saved measurements in separate conversations. Start with a short answer, then open details and source links. Optional internet research helps with current planning and installation-cost questions.

Keep your work

Properties and conversations stay on your device. Export a readable backup to Files or another app, then restore it on another installation. No LandO account is required.

Your assistant, your choice

Choose how LandO answers

Use your own Gemini or OpenAI API key, or download the optional Qwen assistant on a compatible 64-bit Android device. Mapping works without setting up an AI provider.

  • The local model download is about 508 MiB and needs available storage and memory. Smaller models may be slower or less accurate.
  • Cloud providers may charge for API and search use. Cloud questions share recent conversation and property context with your chosen provider after consent.
  • Internet research is enabled by default and can be disabled in AI settings. Local Qwen answers work offline after downloading the model when research is disabled.
  • Maps, address searches, cloud answers and online research need connectivity. Saved geometry and calculations remain available locally.
For planning, with professional judgment. Map-derived boundaries and measurements are estimates. AI answers and online prices may be wrong or outdated. Confirm surveys, ownership, permits, engineering decisions and contractor costs with qualified professionals.
