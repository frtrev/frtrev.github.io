Map and explore

Find addresses, explore satellite maps and edit property boundaries. Add a point with one tap between existing points, lock coordinates to protect your layout, and return to your explicitly saved map view. Location permission is optional.

Measure and arrange

Save areas and distance paths per property, use imperial or metric units, and move, resize or rotate custom areas. Use the ruler for a temporary path: tap its last point to finish, tap the measurement to edit, or tap the ruler to clear it.

Ask LandO

With Pro demo, discuss your property and saved measurements through LandO’s managed Gemini assistant after accepting the account AI agreement. Open calculations, details and source links, and continue longer questions with Keep waiting.

Keep your work

Sign in to an account with its own local property and conversation storage. Export a readable backup and restore it on another installation. Signing in does not synchronize your work between devices.

Accounts & plans

Mapping with Free, managed AI with Pro demo

Create an email/password account and choose a plan. Free supports one saved property without AI. Pro demo supports unlimited properties and the managed LandO assistant.

  • Free and Pro demo are selectable demo plans. This build has no purchases, billing or verified paid subscriptions. Change plans in Settings → Account & plan.
  • The Pro assistant uses LandO’s authenticated backend and Google Gemini. No personal API key or model download is needed. Questions can share recent conversation and property context, including precise coordinates.
  • Read and accept the account AI agreement before sending your first request. Decline to continue mapping, or review and withdraw permission in Settings → AI agreement.
  • Sign-in, maps, address search and managed AI require connectivity. Properties and chats stay in account-specific local storage, with backups for moving them between installations.
  • Dismiss map hints individually, or check Show hints in Settings to see them again.
For planning, with professional judgment. Map-derived boundaries and measurements are estimates. AI answers and online prices may be wrong or outdated. Confirm surveys, ownership, permits, engineering decisions and contractor costs with qualified professionals.
