Industrial utility · Android & iOS

Industrial Alarm Messenger

An authenticated, local notification appliance that lets trusted PLC, SCADA, MES, utility, and facility monitoring systems send group-based alarms through a dedicated Android phone or an approved iPhone deployment.

Designed for controlled industrial use. No advertising, analytics, cloud account, subscription, or external SMS gateway.

Android gateway

Runs an authenticated TCP gateway as a visible foreground service and sends alarms through the device’s SMS service. Supports multipart messaging, SIM status, and dual-SIM selection.

iPhone support

Manages groups and recipient authorization and, on approved deployments, sends unattended SMS through Apple’s Critical Messaging API. Continuous background alarm intake uses Apple’s Local Push Connectivity provider.

iPhone deployment requirements. Critical SMS requires iOS 18.2 or newer, explicit authorization for each recipient, and Apple’s restricted Critical Messaging entitlement. Continuous local alarm intake while the app is backgrounded additionally requires Apple’s restricted Local Push Connectivity entitlement.
