Industrial and emergency disclaimer

Safety Disclaimer

Industrial Alarm Messenger is a best-effort notification gateway. It is not a safety-instrumented-system component, certified emergency communications service, fire alarm panel, machine interlock, or substitute for required life-safety systems.

Delivery limitations

SMS acceptance does not guarantee carrier delivery, recipient receipt, or response. Mobile networks, SIM service, operating-system background policies, Wi-Fi, device hardware, configuration, Apple entitlement or recipient-authorization status, and electrical power can fail or delay messages.

Required safeguards

Safety shutdowns and protective actions must remain in properly designed and certified control equipment. The deploying organization must conduct commissioning tests, periodic proof tests, background-operation tests, supervision, maintenance, and independent escalation procedures appropriate to facility risk.

Operational responsibility

Force-stopping the Android app terminates its foreground gateway. On iPhone, continuous background intake depends on an installed and authorized Local Push Connectivity provider. After a device restart, update, loss of power, or maintenance, verify the platform-specific gateway or provider state and perform an end-to-end test. Maintain alternative alarm and escalation paths for critical conditions.
