Privacy Policy

Brick Breaker is published by Francisco Trevino under NeoTheOne. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Information stored on your device

The game stores settings and gameplay information locally on the player’s device. This can include the high score, control preferences, gameplay options, tutorial state, and music or sound-effect preferences. This information is used only to provide game functionality and is not transmitted to the developer or third parties.

Motion sensor

If the player chooses tilt controls, the game reads the device accelerometer to move the paddle. Accelerometer readings are processed only while the game is running, are not stored, and are not transmitted off the device. Players can use finger-drag or touch-zone controls instead.

Services and permissions

The game does not contain advertising, analytics, user accounts, location tracking, or in-app purchases. It does not request access to contacts, photos, the camera, microphone, or location.

Children’s privacy

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other players.

Changes and contact

If a future version changes these practices, this policy will be updated before that version is released. Questions may be sent to ftrevino@yahoo.com.
