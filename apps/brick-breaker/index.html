About the game

Smash bricks. Claim power-ups.

Brick Breaker brings classic paddle-and-ball arcade action to your phone with colorful levels, responsive controls, and powerful upgrades.

Keep the ball in play, smash increasingly challenging brick layouts, and chase your highest score. Collect extra balls, extend your paddle, and unleash spear shots to clear stubborn bricks before you run out of lives.

Choose the control style that works best for you: drag the paddle directly with your finger, use touch zones for left and right movement, or tilt your phone to steer.

Customize the game speed, controls, power-up behavior, music, and sound effects from the settings menu. Your high score and preferences are saved locally on your device.

Gameplay

Classic action, more ways to play

Progress through damageable brick layouts with extra-ball, extended-paddle, and spear power-ups. Music and sound effects bring each launch, hit, and power-up to life.

Controls

Touch, drag, or tilt

Move the paddle with touch zones, direct finger dragging, or the device accelerometer. Tap to launch the ball and fire spears when the power-up is active.

Progress

Built for another run

Your high score, control preferences, audio settings, and gameplay options are saved locally on your device.

Privacy

No ads or tracking

Brick Breaker has no advertising, analytics, accounts, or in-app purchases. Gameplay information stays on your device and is never sent to NeoTheOne or third parties.
