Sheet Music Rookie Support

Turn a melody into sheet music, then edit, play, and practice it offline.

Get started

  1. Tap Create. Choose Treble, Bass, Alto, or Tenor clef, then an octave range.
  2. Set tempo, key, and time signature for your melody. Start recording when ready and allow microphone access.
  3. Sing one clear note at a time in a quiet room. Pause to edit pitches, durations, rests, ties, and lyrics. Detection is an estimate; review the score.
  4. Save and name your composition. Use Sheets on the home screen to reopen, preview, share, or remove saved scores.
  5. Open compatible MusicXML or MuseScore files from another app with Sheet Music Rookie.

Add and edit chords

  1. Tap a note, then tap the piano button in the score tools to open the chord picker.
  2. Choose the root (A–G), accidental (natural, sharp, or flat), and type (Major, Minor, 7, Major 7, or Minor 7).
  3. Choose a duration and a chord octave: 15 down, 8 down, Standard, 8 up, or 15 up. This octave setting is independent of the score’s octave range.
  4. Tap Save chord. Its name appears above the score and its notes play with the selected instrument. Open the picker again to change or remove it. Chord edits support undo and redo.

Import scores with chord symbols

Open a compatible MuseScore 3/4 file (.mscz or .mscx) or MusicXML score with Sheet Music Rookie. The first melody staff and voice are imported, together with supported chord symbols. Major, minor, dominant seventh, major seventh, and minor seventh symbols are supported. Imported chord duration extends to the next symbol or the end of its measure. Re-import the original file to recover chords from a score previously imported with an older app version.

Balance playback volumes

Open Volumes to adjust music playback, chords, and the metronome separately. Turn Chords down to balance accompaniment against the melody, or set it to 0% to mute chords. The default chord volume is 60%. Confirm with OK, then save the composition to retain its settings. Reopening a saved score restores its volumes; changing one score does not set a global volume for every score.

Microphone troubleshooting

On iOS, check Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone, or the app’s Settings entry. On Android, check Settings → Apps → Sheet Music Rookie → Permissions → Microphone. Also check the system microphone privacy switch. Close other recording apps, enable access, then return and tap Retry in the Microphone unavailable card below the score controls. Reopen the score or restart if needed. Editing works without microphone permission.

Frequently asked questions

Does it need internet or an account?

Core notation, local saving, playback, and practice work offline with no account.

Does it record my voice to a file?

No microphone audio file is saved. Notes and timing derived from your performance can be stored in a composition. Phrase analysis temporarily buffers audio in memory.

Why is a note or rhythm wrong?

Use a quiet room, sing one note at a time, choose the correct tempo and range, and adjust detection settings. Polyphonic music and noisy recordings are not reliable inputs. Review and edit the result.

Where are my scores?

In local app storage. Save before leaving and share a copy before clearing data or deleting the app. There is no cloud account recovery.

How do I remove data?

Clear app storage on Android or use Delete App on iOS. Exported and backed-up copies must be removed separately. You can remove individual saved scores from Sheets on the home screen.
